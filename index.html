[ 01 ] Our mission

We take breakthrough research out of the university lab and turn it into companies that reach patients, with the IP, the team and the capital built in from day one.

Most venture firms wait for a pitch deck. We start with the science, buy it, protect it and build the business around it.

Then we run the company ourselves, with purpose, speed and might, until it can stand on its own.

Every company we build starts in a university lab.

A breakthrough that nobody has built a company around yet. That is where we begin.

What we do

  1. Stage

    01

    Search

    We scour university research for breakthrough science with a clear path to patients or industry.

  2. Stage

    02

    Secure

    We acquire the technology and lock down the intellectual property.

  3. Stage

    03

    Form

    We incorporate the company, put in early-stage capital and set brutal milestones.

  4. Stage

    04

    Build

    We hire the team, often the scientists who developed the work, and act as the hands-on management until it scales.

  5. Stage

    05

    Launch

    We take the product to market and the company to its exit: public listing, strategic sale or standalone growth.
